# Curriculum rules

- Each subject explains object, actors, economic purpose, mechanism, risks and common misconceptions.
- Ensure content is factual, non-promotional, age-appropriate and sourced.
- Numeric stories use fixed fictional values with visible disclaimers.
- Do not confuse a security token, company share, currency exchange, commodity and wager.
- Sports betting is risk literacy only; no services, odds tools, wager simulations, betting instructions, how-to content or referrals.
- Don't claim legal/regulatory status or current market rates without verified sources.
